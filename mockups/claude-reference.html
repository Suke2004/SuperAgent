Thought for 4s ⌄

Retries use exponential backoff with jitter, capped at four attempts:

  • 500 ms base, doubling each attempt, ±20% jitter
  • Only 429 and 5xx re-enter the loop
  • A mid-stream disconnect resumes from the last event id

Thinking… ⌃

The stream is torn down and the turn re-enters the retry loop, but with one important difference: the partial text you've already seen is kept and the resumed response is appended from the last event id.

If the retry budget is exhausted, the turn is marked rate_limited and the composer offers a one-tap switch to your fallback provider
